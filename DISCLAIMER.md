# Disclaimer

Aurum is a **financial market analysis and research tool** written in Rust, built for education and demonstration.

## Not investment advice

- Aurum does NOT execute trades, place broker orders, or manage real money.
- Anything Aurum produces (prices, indicators, signals, rule scores) is informational only.
- "Signals" and "signal strength" come from simple, reproducible rules. They are **not** predictions, guarantees, or recommendations.
- Never describe rule-based output as AI/ML. Treat any forecasting claim as unsubstantiated unless a real, reproducible backtest backs it.

## Market data licensing

- Aurum software is separate from third-party market data.
- DEMO mode uses bundled synthetic fixture data and is clearly labeled as such.
- HISTORICAL/LIVE modes require the user's own provider access and credentials.
- Aurum does not own or redistribute third-party market data; commercial use must comply with the respective provider license.
